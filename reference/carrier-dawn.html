The melody, chords and key path are original. Only the general 80s cinematic style was used as a reference.

  • Pure tuning that moves with the chords. Every note is retuned to a whole-number ratio of the chord it sits on (5:4 thirds, 3:2 fifths), so chords ring smooth without the slight beating of normal tuning. When the chord changes, the same note can shift by a few cents, and the tracker shows how far.
  • Overtone arpeggios. Instead of the triad, the arpeggios race up the natural harmonic series of each chord's root. That includes the soft "natural seventh" (7:4), which sits between a normal minor and major seventh and is never played on a piano.
  • Glass lead. The SID pulse lead is blended with two triangle waves a pure fifth apart, ring-modulated together. It gives a hollow, bell-like tone, a trick the real SID chip could do with its ring-mod switch.
