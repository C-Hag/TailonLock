fullburn-intro.wav: the opening film's music, a cut of the main theme, Full Burn "Arp sound DMX10"
(music/fullburn/), from its ear-safe mix (the bell clock softened, the lead's 2.4 kHz dip, -20 LUFS). Half a second of silence, then bars 1-40 of the
song (6-frame sixteenths on the PAL clock, 1.9152 s a bar), faded over 2.5 s; 32 kHz stereo. The game plays
it from the recording instead of a score (music.c, themes with a .wav), once, and the menu's own theme
takes over when it ends.

The game plays the score (fullburn-intro.json) when it is there, not this recording: a score always wins over
a recording of the same theme (music.c, load_recording). The opening film itself plays heartbeat-intro.

All our own audio: the SID chips, our 80s-rig synths and our own drum machine (h2h-drum). No samples,
no attribution needed.
